About us

Halden Motor Works started in 2009 with two bays and one rule: tell the customer the truth about their car.

Today we run eight bays, but the rule has not changed. We are independent, so we recommend what your car needs, not what hits a sales target. Every vehicle is assigned to one technician from check-in to hand-back, and you can ask them anything.

We work on most European and Japanese makes, with particular depth in performance and premium models. Our workshop is kept clean enough to eat in, because careful work starts with a careful environment.
